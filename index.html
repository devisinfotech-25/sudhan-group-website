History

The journey so far

About Sudhan Group

Every great journey begins with a small step. This visiting card is a reminder of where our story began, the challenges we overcame, and the dreams that continue to inspire us.

  1. 2004

    Learning the Craft

    Learned mobile service in Coimbatore, marking the beginning of a lifelong passion for technology.

    In 2004, he developed hands-on expertise in mobile servicing at Coimbatore, building the technical foundation for future ventures in the mobile and electronics industry.

  2. 2005

    Humble Beginnings

    Launched operations in India as a storekeeper and expanded to Malaysia.

    Started with resilience and entrepreneurial passion, building the foundation for future global growth.

  3. 2009

    Singapore Chapter

    Relocated to Singapore and began as a labourer.

    Showcased perseverance and adaptability while adjusting to a new country and culture — a true turning point.

  4. 2010

    Univercell Technologies Partnership

    Began Singapore chapter under Univercell Technologies Pte Ltd with his sister Sobiya Chandrasekar.

    Joined a group under Univercell Technologies Pte Ltd, marking a significant milestone in his Singapore business journey through a partnership built on trust and family collaboration.

  5. 2015

    Univercell Electronics & Hardware PTE LTD

    Established Univercell Electronics & Hardware in Singapore.

    Expanded into electronics retail and hardware, strengthening the foundation for international business presence.

  6. 2018

    AZ Digital World PTE LTD

    Formed AZ Digital World PTE LTD in Singapore.

    Strengthened digital and online business presence, laying groundwork for the tech-driven ventures to come.

  7. 2019

    Sudhan Infotech – Tamil Nadu

    Established first IT branch in Thiruthuraipundi.

    Founded S Infotech to deliver technology-driven services and enhance local engagement.

  8. 2022

    SPN Educational Trust

    Launched a non-profit for education and community growth.

    Established SPN Educational Trust, focusing on education, empowerment, and community development.

  9. 2023

    Acceleration & Diversification

    Expanded into multiple sectors with new ventures in Singapore.

    • Sudhan Mobiles PTE LTD
    • AZZ Enterprises PTE LTD

    This marked a major leap into digital commerce and mobile retail innovation.

  10. 2025

    Brand Consolidation & Lifestyle Expansion

    March 15 – Launch of Sudhan Infotech Pvt. Ltd.

    Launch of Sudhan Mobiles

    Expanded Sudhan Mobiles across Tamil Nadu, establishing outlets in Chennai, Sathyamangalam, Kovilpatti, and Tiruppur.

    Launched Sudhan Hotspot Salon, marking the group's entry into the grooming and lifestyle industry.

    AZZ Enterprises: A Unified Business Platform

    Consolidated group businesses under AZZ Enterprises, expanded electronics retail and mobile franchise operations through Universal Market and AZ Mobiles, introduced flexible EMI financing, and launched PayLater.sg for digital financing applications.

  11. 2030

    Vision for Global Leadership

    Focused on global growth and sustainable innovation.

    Actively seeking investors to scale internationally, aiming for leadership in innovation and sustainable business impact.
